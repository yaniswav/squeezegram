Too big for Telegram?
Squeeze it. Send it.

squeezegram is a free Windows app that compresses videos over Telegram's upload limit to just under it, then sends them to your chats, groups and topics from your own account.

Download for Windows View on GitHub

Free · Windows 10 and 11, x64 · Unofficial app, not affiliated with Telegram

Just under the limit. Nothing more.

The limit is about 2 GB, or about 4 GB if your account has Telegram Premium. squeezegram reads which one applies from your account.

Compressed on your PC

On your graphics card when it can (NVENC, Quick Sync or AMF), otherwise on the processor. Files already under the limit are sent as they are.

Sent from your account

To chats, groups and forum topics, with caption templates, thumbnails and duplicate detection. Sign in with a QR code or your phone number.

A queue you can trust

One upload at a time. Pause, cancel, reorder, and recover after a crash. A file whose upload is uncertain is never resent without asking you.

Your files stay yours

No analytics, no telemetry, no server of its own. Location and camera data can be removed from photos, and from videos sent without re-encoding.

Free is complete. Premium adds more.

Everything you need to compress and send is free. squeezegram Premium is an optional one-time purchase.

Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

squeezegram is not code-signed, so SmartScreen warns about it, and some antivirus programs may flag unsigned software by mistake.

Download it only from the GitHub releases page. Each release lists the SHA-256 of its files in SHA256SUMS.txt; check yours with Get-FileHash .\squeezegram-<version>-setup-x64.exe. If it matches, choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

Does squeezegram upload my files anywhere?

Only to Telegram, and only what you put in the queue. squeezegram has no server of its own and no telemetry. It contacts GitHub to download ffmpeg once and to check for updates (you can turn that off).

Details are in the privacy notice.

Can Telegram restrict my account?

Sending from a user account is subject to Telegram's terms. Sending to groups where you lack permissions, or many files in a short time, can get an account limited. squeezegram waits as long as Telegram asks and lets you set an interval per destination, but it cannot prevent a restriction. You use it at your own risk.

What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (x64) and a Telegram account. On first launch, squeezegram offers to download ffmpeg (an LGPL build hosted on this project's releases, checked by SHA-256), or you can point it at your own.

Is squeezegram Premium the same as Telegram Premium?

No. Telegram Premium is Telegram's own subscription, which raises the upload limit to about 4 GB. squeezegram Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks the extra settings and features of this app.

Can I see the code?

Yes. The source is public on GitHub under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 (source-available, free for noncommercial use). You can build it yourself.
